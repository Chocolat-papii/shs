Services

At Solly’s Handyman Services, we provide expert solutions for your home or business. From fitted kitchens and built-in wardrobes to bespoke cabinetry, every project is handled with precision and care.

Our team focuses on premium finishes, accurate material planning, and clean, respectful workmanship — ensuring your space is transformed efficiently, beautifully, and exactly as you envisioned.

Fitted Kitchens

As the saying goes, The kitchen is the heart of the home. At Solly's we get that. We’re here to bring your vision to life with craftsmanship that transforms your kitchen into the true heart of your home.

We offer a variety of premium finishes, including:

  • Melamine
  • Gloss
  • Laminated/Wrap Options

Bedroom Essentials

Maximize your space while adding a touch of elegance to your bedroom. We create custom bedroom cabinetry that seamlessly blend functionality with your personal style.

With a range of finishes and layouts, we ensure your wardrobe is as beautiful as it is practical.

  • Built-in Wardrobes
  • Dressing Drawers
  • Bedside Pedestals
  • Walk-in Clauset

Ceiling Installations

We also offer expertly installed PVC, Rhino board, and Nu-tech ceilings to complement living rooms, bedrooms, and all interior spaces.

Our ceiling installations ensure a seamless, polished finish, adding the perfect touch to your newly transformed space.

General Improvements

We handle a wide range of home improvement tasks with precision and care, including TV stands, door installations, painting, and tiling.

Our team ensures every job is completed efficiently, with clean workmanship and attention to detail, leaving your space looking flawless.

Our Process

Our process is designed to be as seamless and straight forward as possible

1
Initial Consultation

We discuss your vision, requirements, and budget to understand your goals and preferences.

2
Site Inspection

Our team visits your space to take accurate measurements and assess any challenges or opportunities.

3
Planning and Design

We create detailed plans and design solutions that meet your needs, style, and functionality requirements.

4
Customized Quotation

Receive a clear, detailed quote that outlines costs, materials, and timelines for your approval.

5
Execution

Our skilled team carries out the project with precision, keeping your space clean and organized throughout.

6
Quality Assurance

We rigorously inspect all work to ensure it meets our high standards and your expectations.

7
Final Walkthrough

We guide you through the completed project, addressing any final questions and ensuring your satisfaction.

You proclaim, We create.

We believe every space should be functional, well-crafted, and thoughtfully finished. Whether you’re looking for timeless elegance or practical comfort within your budget, we work closely with you to bring your vision to life — with precision, care, and attention to detail.
