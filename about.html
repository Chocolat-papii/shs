Our Brief History

About Us

Meet Solly, founder of Solly’s Handyman Services. With over four decades at the forefront of the fitted cabinetry industry, Solly has built a reputation for exceptional craftsmanship, specializing in fitted kitchens, built-in wardrobes, and ceiling installations.

Over time, the business naturally evolved, expanding its expertise to include comprehensive home repairs and refined living room enhancements — always with the same commitment to quality and detail.

From the very beginning, our commitment to world-class standards has set us apart. Every project we undertake reflects our meticulous attention to detail and unwavering dedication to client satisfaction. This pursuit of excellence has earned us the trust of our clients, resulting in a strong base of returning customers — a true testament to the quality and reliability of our work.

Our Company Values

We don’t chase trends — we focus on timeless quality, functional design, and results that stand the test of time. That’s why our clients return, and why our work continues to speak for itself.

Mission

At Solly's, our mission is to deliver exceptional handyman solutions that meet and exceed the expectations of our clients. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, integrity, and professionalism in every project we undertake.

Through continuous improvement and innovation, we aim to provide services that are not only efficient and reliable but also tailored to the unique needs of each client. Our mission is to uphold the legacy of excellence and meticulous attention to detail that has earned us the trust and loyalty of our clients.

Vision

Our vision is to be the leading provider of high-quality handyman services, renowned for our craftsmanship, reliability, and client satisfaction. We aspire to expand our offerings and incorporate our extensive experience into creating luxurious, bespoke furniture that reflects our commitment to excellence.

By fostering a culture of continuous improvement and innovation, we aim to set new benchmarks in the industry and build lasting relationships with our clients based on trust and superior service.
